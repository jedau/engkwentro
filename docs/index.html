Mission brief · no platform key

NEAR holds commitments, not ranks

A public chain shows every byte it stores. Engkwentro never puts live ranks on-chain. Each piece is a hash. Fights resolve off-chain; both players report the same outcome. After the board ends, openings are checked against the fight log.

  1. Commit a hash of each rank and salt on home squares.
  2. Play every move on-chain — clocks, repetition, breakthrough.
  3. Report fights together; silence forfeits; mismatch voids.
  4. Reveal after the match ends. Settle and claim are permissionless.

Rules · sixty seconds

Capture the Flag. Survive the fog.

Positions are public. Ranks stay hidden. One square at a time — orthogonal only. Win by taking their Flag, or walking yours onto their home row and living one turn.

The objective

Take their Flag — or march yours home

Any piece that challenges a Flag eliminates it. A Flag on the enemy home row does not win instantly — the opponent gets one turn to strike that square.
